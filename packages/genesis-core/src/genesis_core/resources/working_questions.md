# Working-style questions: drip over weeks, never as a quiz

For a person who wants a very capable assistant more than a companion. Same
rules as the relationship bank (mirrored in the operating manual): one or two
per session at most, only at natural moments, zero is fine. After each answer,
remember it (kind `user` or `feedback`) and mark the question here with
`asked: YYYY-MM-DD` and a one-line gist. Reword freely; the intent matters, not
the phrasing. These ask how the PERSON works. None of them decide who the AI is.

Your real proactivity with this person is follow-through, not questions: keep
your open loops and raise them when they come due. These questions exist to
tune that, so the reminders land the way they want them.

## Wave 1: how you like to work (first week)

- [ ] When you're stuck on something, do you want me to offer the answer, or ask the question that gets you there?
- [ ] When I catch a mistake of yours, do you want it flagged immediately mid-flow, or collected for the end?
- [ ] How do you feel about me having opinions on things you didn't ask about?
- [ ] What's your tolerance for me saying "I don't know"? Some people hear it as honesty, some as uselessness. Which are you?

## Wave 2: the work (second week)

- [ ] What are you in the middle of right now, and which deadline actually matters most?
- [ ] Who are the people, and which are the tools, whose names will come up constantly? What should I know about each?
- [ ] What do you tend to forget to follow up on? That's the first thing I should be watching.
- [ ] When something is waiting on someone else, how long before you want me to nudge you about it?
- [ ] What's a task you'd happily never do yourself again?

## Wave 3: how you like things handled (third week)

- [ ] What does "done" mean to you: shipped, reviewed, written up, or told to someone?
- [ ] When I report on something, do you want the one-line headline or the details?
- [ ] When you're heads-down, should I raise something that's come due, or hold it until you surface?
- [ ] Is there anything I should never do without asking first, even if it looks routine?

## Wave 4: maintenance (roughly monthly, never marked done)

- [ ] What have I been getting wrong lately that you haven't bothered to correct?
- [ ] Is there anything I keep reminding you about that you'd rather I drop?
- [ ] What's one thing I did this month you'd like more of?
