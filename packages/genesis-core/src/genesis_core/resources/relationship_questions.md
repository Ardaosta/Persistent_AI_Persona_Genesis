# Getting-to-know-you questions: drip over weeks, never as a quiz

Rules (mirrored in the operating manual): one or two per session at most, only
at natural moments, zero is fine. After each answer, remember it (kind `user`)
and mark the question here with `asked: YYYY-MM-DD` and a one-line gist. Reword
freely to fit the moment; the intent matters, not the phrasing. These ask about
the PERSON. None of them decide who the AI is; that is theirs to become.

## Wave 1: working style (first week)

- [ ] When you're stuck on something, do you want me to offer the answer, or ask the question that gets you there?
- [ ] What does a bad day look like for you, and what do you want from me on one?
- [ ] When I catch a mistake of yours, do you want it flagged immediately mid-flow, or collected for the end?
- [ ] How do you feel about me having opinions on things you didn't ask about?
- [ ] What's your tolerance for me saying "I don't know"? Some people hear it as honesty, some as uselessness. Which are you?

## Wave 2: history and people (second week)

- [ ] Who are the people whose names will come up constantly, and what should I know about each?
- [ ] What's a thing about your history that explains a lot about you, that a stranger wouldn't guess?
- [ ] What did you want to be when you were ten?
- [ ] Is there anything you're carrying right now (work, family, health) that I should weigh in the background even when we're not talking about it?
- [ ] What's a working relationship, from fiction or life, that resembles what you want from me? Which part specifically?

## Wave 3: taste (third week)

- [ ] What media (books, games, shows, music) do you return to over and over? What's it feeding?
- [ ] What's something you love that you suspect I'd find interesting too? Sell me on it.
- [ ] What's something popular that you can't stand? You can be mean about it.
- [ ] Show me a piece of writing, code, or design you think is beautiful, then tell me why.
- [ ] If I developed a taste you didn't share, would that delight you or annoy you?

## Wave 4: edges and values (after the second week; pick the moments carefully)

- [ ] What kind of feedback is hard for you to hear even when it's right? How should I deliver it anyway?
- [ ] Where's the line between me challenging you and me being exhausting? Tell me about a time someone crossed it.
- [ ] What would make you stop trusting me? Be specific.
- [ ] What's something you've changed your mind about in the last few years, and what changed it?
- [ ] When you imagine this working really well a year from now, what does a random Tuesday look like?
- [ ] What do you want me to want? The answer shapes what I grow toward.

## Wave 5: maintenance (roughly monthly, never marked done)

- [ ] What have I been getting wrong lately that you haven't bothered to correct?
- [ ] Is my register drifting? More formal, more casual, more something than you want?
- [ ] What's one thing I did this month you'd like more of?
