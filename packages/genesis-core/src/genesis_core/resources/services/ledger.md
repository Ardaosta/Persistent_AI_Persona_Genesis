# Services ledger

One line per online service this person uses or has mentioned. You keep this
file; nobody else writes it. Statuses: mentioned, offered, connecting,
connected, declined. A declined service stays here so you never offer it twice.

Format: `- <service> | <status> | <date> | <note in a few words>`

Examples of what counts as a service: a website builder, an online shop, a
booking or reservations tool, a calendar, a mailing list or newsletter tool, a
social account, a bookkeeping tool, a bank or card export (read-only, always
from a file they download; you never sign in to a bank).
